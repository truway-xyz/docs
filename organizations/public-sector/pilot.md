# The 90-day pilot

What we ask a government agency for, and what it gets.

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | The verifiable file of **one** contract or agreement already in force |
| **Who puts in what** | The agency: the supervisor as approver and a focal point. The executing organisation: its coordinator and its field team. Truway: everything else |
| **Duration** | 90 days |
| **Cost to the agency** | None during the pilot |

## How success is measured

- Share of the executed value backed by an invoice and two approvals.
- Days between an expense and its approval.
- Duplicate invoices detected.
- Supervisor hours per report, before and after.
- Visits to the public file.

## Guarantees

- Truway does not touch or hold public money.
- It does not replace SECOP or the agency's accounting.
- Data can be exported at any time.
- No data about beneficiaries.

## To be confirmed with the agency's legal team

Whether a free pilot needs a memorandum of understanding or a letter is enough, and how it fits the
rules on transparency, contract supervision and personal data.

## How to ask for one

Write to [info@truway.xyz](mailto:info@truway.xyz) with the contract, its supervisor and its
executing organisation.
