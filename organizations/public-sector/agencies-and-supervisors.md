# For agencies and supervisors

> Status: approvals are **live on testnet**. Linking contracts from SECOP II and alerts are **in
> design**.

## What the agency puts in

- The **supervisor** of the contract, who approves budget lines and expenses.
- A **focal point** who follows the pilot.

Truway does the rest. The agency does not install software, change its procedures or hand over
any system.

## What the supervisor does

1. **Approves the budget lines** of the investment plan once. From then on, any change needs a new
   approval and leaves a trace.
2. **Approves each expense** filed by the coordinator, seeing the invoice, the budget line, its
   account code and what is left in it.
3. **Rejects** an expense when something is wrong. The rejection is recorded too; recording the
   reason is planned.

The supervisor can never approve an expense they filed themselves.

## What the supervisor sees

| | |
|---|---|
| Contracted against executed, per budget line | Live on testnet |
| Every expense with its invoice, supplier, who filed it and when | Live on testnet |
| Alert: the expense is more than what is left in the line | Design (today the expense is refused) |
| Alert: this invoice was already reported in another contract | Design |
| Reconciliation: "the procurement portal reports paid X; the file has approved Y" | Design |

## What the agency gets

- Evidence for each payment it authorises.
- Less time spent putting reports together: the execution report comes out of the file.
- A public file that oversight groups and comptrollers can check without asking anyone.
