# FAQ

<details>
<summary>Do I need to install anything?</summary>

No. The supervisor and the coordinator use the web app; the field team uses WhatsApp.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does Truway replace SECOP?</summary>

No. SECOP publishes the contract; Truway shows how it is executed.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Who sees the invoices?</summary>

The file stays private with the organization. The public record keeps only its fingerprint, which proves which file was approved without revealing it.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What if someone makes a mistake?</summary>

A pending expense can be rejected, and the rejection is recorded. An approved expense cannot be edited or deleted.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can the same invoice be used twice?</summary>

Not within the same organization. Checking across organizations is on the [roadmap](roadmap.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does Truway handle public money?</summary>

No. Payments are made by the agency and the organization through their banks.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How much does it cost?</summary>

The 90-day pilot costs the agency nothing. Prices will be published once they are final.
</details>
