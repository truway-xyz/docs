# Business model

## Open core

| Piece | License |
|---|---|
| Proof of Spend, the standard | Apache-2.0 |
| `budget-treasury`, the reference contract | Apache-2.0 |
| These docs | CC BY 4.0 |
| The Truway app | Closed source |

A record that follows the standard can be read without Truway, and data can be exported at any time.

## Customers

| Customer | What they get |
|---|---|
| **Executing organization** | Accounts for every contract without retyping invoices, with one report per funder |
| **Government agency** | Supervision with evidence and a public file per contract |
| **Funders and international cooperation** | Execution per funding source, as it happens |

## How it starts

With a [90-day pilot](../public-sector/truway-for-agencies.md) at no cost to the agency: one contract, its supervisor and its executing organization.

## How it grows

* More contracts per agency, then more agencies.
* **Country packs**: chart of accounts, electronic invoice, exchange rate and local rails. Colombia first, with a generic IFRS pack.
* Main metric: pesos traced end to end.

Prices will be published once they are final.
