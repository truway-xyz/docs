# Roadmap

## Next

* **Per-person signing** from the browser, without handling keys.
* **SECOP II contracts**: link a contract by its ID and fill in its data automatically.
* **One invoice, once, across organizations**, with electronic invoices identified by their CUFE.
* **Alerts for the supervisor**: expense over the line and invoice already reported.
* **Budget lines managed from the app.**

## Then

* **Field advances as money** through the Stellar Disbursement Platform, accounted for with receipts, with unspent funds returned.
* **Cash-out to pesos** with a local partner.
* **Contract governance**: several admins, timelock, expiring proposals and a pause.
* **Independent security review** and mainnet launch.

## Later

* Proof of Spend `v1.0` with a reference indexer.
* SECOP I.
* Exports for public accounting and comptrollers.
* More country packs.
