# For executing organisations

> Status: **live on testnet** with demo data.

You run a contract or agreement funded with public money. Truway lets you account for it as you
go, without retyping invoices and without a different report for each funder.

## Set up the contract

1. Create the programme and load the **budget lines** of the investment plan, each with its
   **account code** (for example PUC 5135) and its **funding source**.
2. Invite a **second person** to approve. Nothing becomes final with one person alone.
3. Create your **field teams** and register the phone number of each team lead.

## Give a field advance

1. On the budget line, choose **Assign funds**, pick the team and the amount.
2. A second person approves it.
3. The line shows the advance as *Registered · verifiable*.

## Account for a purchase from the field

1. The team lead pays the supplier and sends a **photo of the receipt** to the Truway WhatsApp
   number.
2. The assistant replies with the amount, supplier and date it read, and asks **which budget line**
   it belongs to.
3. The team lead answers with the number of the line. The expense goes to approval.
4. Someone other than the person who filed it approves it in the app.

The assistant suggests, it never decides: every expense waits for a person's approval.

## Reports

- **Execution per budget line and funding source**, for each funder.
- **General ledger** with debit and credit accounts, downloadable as CSV for your accounting
  software.
- A **public link** to the programme you can send to the agency or to any funder.
