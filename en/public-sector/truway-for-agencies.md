# Truway for government agencies

Truway gives the agency a verifiable file for every contract a third party executes. The supervisor approves with evidence, and oversight groups and comptrollers read the file without asking anyone.

## What the supervisor does

1. **Approves the budget lines** of the investment plan. Any later change needs a new approval.
2. **Approves each expense** the executing organization proposes, with the invoice, line, account and available balance in view.
3. **Rejects** what does not add up. The rejection is recorded too.

The supervisor can never approve something they proposed.

## What the agency sees

| | Availability |
|---|---|
| Contracted against spent, per budget line and per funder | Available |
| Every expense with its invoice, supplier, who proposed it and when | Available |
| General ledger as CSV | Available |
| Contract pulled from SECOP II by its ID | Coming soon |
| Alert for an invoice already reported in another contract | Coming soon |
| Reconciliation with what SECOP reports as paid | Coming soon |

## 90-day pilot

| | |
|---|---|
| **Scope** | The file of one contract or agreement in force |
| **The agency provides** | The supervisor as approver and a focal point |
| **The executing organization provides** | Its coordinator and its field team |
| **Truway provides** | The platform, the setup and support |
| **Duration** | 90 days |
| **Cost to the agency** | None |

**Pilot metrics:** share of the executed value with an invoice and two approvals, days between an expense and its approval, duplicate invoices detected, supervisor hours per report, and visits to the public file.

To request a pilot, write to [info@truway.xyz](mailto:info@truway.xyz) with the contract, its supervisor and its executing organization.

## Guarantees

* Truway does not touch or hold public money.
* It does not replace SECOP or the agency's accounting.
* It does not write into government systems.
* It does not receive beneficiary data.
* Data can be exported whenever the agency wants.

See [Data and privacy](../concepts/data-and-privacy.md) and [Government integrations](integrations.md).
