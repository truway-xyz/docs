# Programs and budget lines

A **program** is the unit of work in Truway. It usually maps to one contract or agreement.

## Budget lines

The program budget is split into **budget lines** from the investment plan. Each line has:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Name | Study materials |
| Account code | PUC `5195` Miscellaneous |
| Funder | Departmental education fund |
| Currency | COP |
| Planned | 500,000 |

In **Program budget** you see, per line, what is **Planned**, **Allocated** to teams, **Available** and **Spent**.

{% hint style="info" %}
For now, the Truway team sets up the budget lines and their accounts with you when the program is activated.
{% endhint %}

## Funding sources

Each budget line has one source: national budget, transfers, royalties, own funds or cooperation. One source's money never pays another source's line, so the report per funder always adds up.

## Field teams

A **team** executes part of the budget in the field. It has a lead and receives advances from budget lines. Its approved expenses feed the line's execution.

## Rules

* A budget line can never spend more than it has.
* Changing a line's budget needs a second person's approval.
* A line's budget cannot go below what was already spent.
