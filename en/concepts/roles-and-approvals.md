# Roles and approvals

Anything that changes the budget or records an expense takes two steps: one person **proposes** and another **approves**. Nobody can approve what they proposed.

## Organization roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Organization admin | Manage people, roles, programs and approvals |
| Project manager | Create programs and set their budget |
| Finance operator | Record expenses, including those that arrive over WhatsApp |
| Finance approver | Approve or reject expenses and allocations |
| Report manager | Prepare and send reports |
| Auditor | Read the full history and audit trail |
| Viewer | Read programs and expenses |

Organizations can combine permissions into their own roles. Roles are assigned in **Assigned roles**, on each person's profile.

## The two-person rule

| Action | Proposes | Approves |
|---|---|---|
| Assign a field advance to a team | Whoever manages the budget | Someone else allowed to approve |
| Record an expense | Whoever records it, in the app or over WhatsApp | Someone else allowed to approve |
| Change a budget line | Whoever manages the budget | Someone else allowed to approve |
| Add or remove people who approve | An admin | Another admin |

If you try to approve something you proposed, the app shows *"You proposed this. Someone else must approve it."*

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for approval** | Proposed, waiting for the second person |
| **Recording…** | The approval is being written to the public record |
| **Registered · verifiable** | On the public record, with its code |
| **Not recorded** | It could not be written; it can be retried |
