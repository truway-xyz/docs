# Record an expense on WhatsApp

The team lead records each purchase from WhatsApp, at the moment of payment. Nothing to install.

**Before you start:** the team lead's number must be enabled. See [Create a program](create-a-program.md).

## Steps

1. Pay the supplier and ask for the receipt or invoice.
2. Take a clear photo of the receipt and send it to the Truway WhatsApp number.
3. The assistant replies with the **amount**, **supplier** and **date** it read, and the list of available budget lines.
4. Reply with the number of the budget line, for example `1`.
5. The assistant confirms that the expense was sent for approval.

The expense appears in **Approvals** for someone else to review.

## Tips for the photo

* The whole invoice inside the frame, no fingers on it.
* Good light, no glare.
* For an electronic invoice, the PDF is better than a photo.

{% hint style="info" %}
AI suggests the fields but does not decide: the expense only counts once someone else approves it.
{% endhint %}
