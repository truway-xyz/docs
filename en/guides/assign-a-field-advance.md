# Assign a field advance

An advance gives part of a budget line to a field team. The team then accounts for it with the receipt of each purchase.

## Propose the advance

1. Open the program and go to **Program budget**.
2. On the budget line, press **Assign funds**.
3. In **Team**, choose the team.
4. In **Amount**, enter the value. Below it you see what is **Available to assign**.
5. Press **Propose allocation**.

The line shows the allocation as **Waiting for a second approval**.

## Approve the advance

Someone else opens the program and, in **Awaiting approval**, presses **Approve**. The allocation moves to **Registered · verifiable** and appears in **Team allocations**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If the amount is more than what is left in the line, the app does not let you propose it: *"That amount is more than what is left in this item."*
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Today the advance is recorded and approved, and the money is handed over through the organization's usual channels. Paying the advance out from Truway is on the [roadmap](../about/roadmap.md).
{% endhint %}
