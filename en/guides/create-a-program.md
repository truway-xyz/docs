# Create a program

## Create the program

1. In the menu, open **Programs**.
2. Press **New program**.
3. In **Create a new program**, fill in:
   * **Program name**, for example *Higher-education scholarships Cundinamarca*.
   * **Total budget** and **Currency**.
4. Press **Create program**.

{% hint style="info" %}
For now, the Truway team sets up the budget lines, their PUC accounts and their funders with you when the program is activated.
{% endhint %}

## Create the field teams

1. In the menu, open **Teams**.
2. Press **New team**.
3. Enter the name (for example *Soacha team*), choose the **Lead** and press **Create team**.

## Invite your organization

1. In **Settings**, open the users section and press **Invite user**.
2. Press **Create invitation**, then **Copy link**.
3. Send the link to the person. Until they use it, they show as **Pending invitation**.
4. Once they sign in, open their profile, choose **Assign role…** and press **Assign**.

You need at least two people who can approve: nothing is recorded with one alone.

## Enable WhatsApp volunteers

1. In **WhatsApp volunteers**, add an optional label and press **Create link**.
2. Share the link with the field team.
3. Each volunteer opens the link: WhatsApp opens with the message ready, they tap **Send** and they are enabled.
