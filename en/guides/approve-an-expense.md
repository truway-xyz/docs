# Approve an expense

## Steps

1. In the menu, open **Approvals**.
2. Open the expense. Check the invoice, the budget line, the account code and what is available.
3. Press **Approve** or **Reject**.

When approved, the expense goes through **Recording…** and then **Registered · verifiable**, with its code.

## What to check

* The amount matches the invoice.
* The budget line is the right one for that purchase.
* The supplier and date make sense for the program.

## Rules

* You cannot approve an expense you recorded: *"You proposed this. Someone else must approve it."*
* An expense cannot exceed what is left in its budget line.
* An invoice already used for another expense of the organization is refused.
* An approved expense cannot be edited or deleted.
