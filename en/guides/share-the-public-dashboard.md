# Share the public dashboard

Every program has a public dashboard with its execution per budget line, per team and per funder. Share it with the agency, funders or any oversight group.

## Open the dashboard

1. Open the program and go to **Program budget**.
2. Press **Public dashboard**.
3. Copy the address from your browser and share it.

The dashboard shows **Spent**, **Assigned to teams**, **Expenses recorded** and **Approved by two people**, plus execution **By funder** and **By budget line and team**.

## Download the general ledger

On the dashboard, press **Download general ledger (CSV)**. For each expense the file includes: date, code, description, budget line, chart of accounts, debit account, credit account, amount, currency, invoice fingerprint and transaction.

It opens in any spreadsheet and can be imported into your accounting software.
