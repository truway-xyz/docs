# How it works

A program in Truway is a contract or agreement. The money is split into budget lines, every movement is approved by a second person, and everything lands on the public record.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/how-it-works.svg" alt="Contract, budget lines with account codes, approval, field advance, approval, purchase in the field with a WhatsApp receipt, expense filed, approval, public record and report."><figcaption><p>The path of a program</p></figcaption></figure>

## 1. Budget

The program is split into **budget lines** from the investment plan. Each line has its PUC account and its funder. See [Programs and budget lines](../concepts/programs-and-budget-lines.md).

## 2. Field advances

The coordinator assigns an advance from a budget line to a field team. Someone else approves it. See [Assign a field advance](../guides/assign-a-field-advance.md).

## 3. Expenses

The team lead pays in the field and sends a photo of the receipt over WhatsApp. The assistant reads the amount, supplier, date and tax ID, and asks which budget line it belongs to. See [Record an expense on WhatsApp](../guides/record-an-expense-on-whatsapp.md).

## 4. Approval

Someone other than the person who recorded the expense approves it in **Approvals**. See [Approve an expense](../guides/approve-an-expense.md).

## 5. Public record

The approved expense lands on the public record with a code such as `PAY-2026-0004`. The program dashboard, the report per line and the general ledger come from there. See [Public record](../concepts/public-record.md).
