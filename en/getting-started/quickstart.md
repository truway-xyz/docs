# Quickstart

In this guide you create a program, give a field advance to a team, record an expense over WhatsApp, approve it and verify it on the public record.

**You need:** an account at [app.truway.xyz](https://app.truway.xyz), a second person in your organization who can approve, and a phone with WhatsApp.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Create the program

In **Programs**, press **New program**. Enter the name, the total budget and the currency, and press **Create program**.

[Create a program](../guides/create-a-program.md)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Invite an approver and create the team

Invite the second person with **Invite user** and give them a role that can approve. In **Teams**, create the field team with **New team**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Give a field advance

In **Program budget**, press **Assign funds** on the budget line, choose the team and the amount, and press **Propose allocation**. The other person approves it.

[Assign a field advance](../guides/assign-a-field-advance.md)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Record an expense on WhatsApp

The team lead sends a photo of the receipt to the Truway number and replies with the number of the budget line.

[Record an expense on WhatsApp](../guides/record-an-expense-on-whatsapp.md)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Approve it

The other person opens **Approvals** and presses **Approve**. The expense moves to **Registered · verifiable**.

[Approve an expense](../guides/approve-an-expense.md)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Verify it

Open [app.truway.xyz/proof](https://app.truway.xyz/proof), type the expense code and press **Verify**.

[Verify an expense](../guides/verify-an-expense.md)
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
