# What is Truway

Truway is the platform for executing and accounting for public money. It is used by the organizations that execute contracts or agreements with the government, by the agency that supervises them, and by anyone who wants to check how the money was spent.

{% hint style="info" %}
Truway is in beta. The app runs in a test environment and the programs you see are demo data.
{% endhint %}

## Who uses it

| Who | What they do in Truway |
|---|---|
| **Executing organization** (non-profit, federation, operator) | Loads the budget, gives field advances and records every expense with its invoice |
| **Government agency** | Approves budget lines and expenses through the contract supervisor |
| **Field team** | Sends a photo of each receipt over WhatsApp |
| **Oversight groups, councils, comptrollers and citizens** | Read the public program file and verify any expense |

## What it includes

* **Budget per line**, each with its account code (PUC) and funding source.
* **Petty cash advances** for field teams, accounted for with receipts.
* **WhatsApp capture**: AI reads the receipt and asks which budget line it belongs to.
* **Two-person approval**: whoever records an expense can never approve it.
* **Single-use invoices**: a document cannot back two expenses of the same organization.
* **Public program file**, with a code per expense to verify it.
* **Execution report and general ledger**, downloadable as CSV.

## What sets it apart

Every approved expense lands on a public record that neither Truway, nor the agency, nor the organization controls. The record follows [Proof of Spend](../proof-of-spend/README.md), an open standard, so any system can read it without Truway.
